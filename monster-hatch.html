    Element matchups

    A strong element deals 1.5× damage. A resisted one deals 0.7×. Attack types appear on the move buttons.

    How to play

    1. 1Hatch an egg to meet a random original monster with its own starting stats.
    2. 2In Weeks 1–3, choose any stat each time, including the same stat repeatedly. Strength adds attack and health, Defense reduces damage, and Speed improves accuracy.
    3. 3In Week 4, choose attacks during a turn-based fight. Element matchups can make a move stronger or weaker.
    4. 4A win adds a level and starts another three-week training cycle. A loss brings a new Level 0 egg.
